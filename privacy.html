Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sudoku app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Sudoku is designed to work offline-first: the app functions fully without an internet connection, and does not require an account.

Information Collected

The app stores your puzzle progress (the current board, notes, elapsed time, difficulty, and hints used) and your app settings (such as timer, haptics, sound, theme, and input preferences) on your device. The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contact list, location, or any other personal information.

How Information Is Used

The puzzle progress and settings saved on your device are used only to let you resume a puzzle in progress and to remember your preferences between sessions. This information is not used for any other purpose.

Data Storage

All puzzle progress and settings are stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage. This data is not transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us. The app can be used fully offline, and this locally stored data never leaves your device as part of normal app use.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or backend/cloud services. The app is distributed through the Apple App Store, and app installation and updates may involve standard Apple/App Store infrastructure, which is outside our control and governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Analytics

The app does not use any analytics tools or services. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or behavioral data from your use of the app.

Advertising

The app does not display advertising and does not integrate any advertising networks or ad identifiers.

Account Information

The app does not require or support user accounts, sign-in, or sign-up. There is no account information to collect.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information, use it by children does not raise the concerns that data-collection practices typically do under regulations such as COPPA. [REPLACE THIS] — If you determine the app is directed at children or add any data collection in the future, update this section accordingly.

Data Retention

Puzzle progress and settings remain stored on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or reset it from within the app (where such an option is provided). Since this information is never sent to us, we do not hold a copy of it and cannot delete it on your behalf — removing the app or its data from your device removes it entirely.

Security

Puzzle progress and settings are stored using your device's standard on-device storage mechanisms and are protected by the security features of your device's operating system. Because this data is not transmitted to a server, it is not exposed to network-based risks during normal app use.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at anton-i-bor@hotmail.com.
