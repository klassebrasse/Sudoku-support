Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sudoku app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Sudoku is designed to work offline-first: the app functions fully without an internet connection, and an account is optional. If you don't sign in, your games and settings stay on your device.

Information Collected

On your device, always. The app stores your puzzle progress (the current board, notes, elapsed time, difficulty and hints used), your game history and statistics (including your daily streak), and your app settings (such as timer, haptics, sound, theme, language and input preferences). If you sign in, your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain).

If you sign in (optional). You can sign in with Apple or Google to back up your games and appear on the leaderboard. We then store:

  • An account identifier and the email address the sign-in provider shares with us. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address. Google may also share your name and profile picture; the app doesn't use or display them.
  • The username you choose.
  • Your game results: for each game, the mode (daily or free play), difficulty, which daily puzzle it was, whether it was completed, your time, mistakes, hints and notes used, number of moves, when it was started and finished, and the app version and platform it was played on.

If you turn on notifications (signed in only). We store a push token for your device, your device's platform, its time zone and your app language, linked to your account, together with your notification preferences and a log of the notifications sent to you.

The app does not ask for or collect your contact list, location, photos, or any other personal information.

How Information Is Used

  • To let you resume a puzzle and remember your preferences.
  • To back up your games, statistics and streak, and restore them when you sign in on another device.
  • To show the daily leaderboard (see below).
  • To send you the daily puzzle reminder and occasional announcements, at a sensible local time and in your language, and to make sure no notification is sent twice.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers listed below, who process it on our behalf to run the app.

Leaderboard

If you're signed in, your first completed attempt at each day's published daily puzzle appears on that day's leaderboard, showing your username, time, mistakes and hints used. This includes daily puzzles you played on the device before signing in, since signing in backs up your existing games. The leaderboard is visible to everyone who uses the app, including people who aren't signed in. Your email address and account identifier are never shown.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on: after you finish a daily puzzle while signed in, the app offers a daily reminder, and your device asks for permission only if you accept. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service. Signing out removes your device's push token from our servers. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device's settings.

Data Storage

Puzzle progress, game history and settings are stored on your device. If you sign in, your account, username, game results and notification data are also stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase. If you don't sign in, nothing about your games is sent to our servers.

The app also downloads the daily puzzles and checks for app updates over the internet. These requests don't include your games or account information, but like any internet request they include standard technical information, such as your IP address and app version, which our service providers may log for security and operations.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: our backend (database, sign-in and server functions), which stores the account data described above.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service, and app distribution through the App Store.
  • Google: Sign in with Google.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications to Apple's service and delivers app updates.

Each provider handles information under its own privacy practices. The app does not integrate any analytics or advertising services.

Analytics

The app does not use any analytics tools or services. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or behavioral data from your use of the app.

Advertising

The app does not display advertising and does not integrate any advertising networks or ad identifiers.

Your Choices

  • You can use the whole app without an account. Nothing about your games leaves your device then.
  • Sign out ends your session on that device and removes its push token from our servers. Your games stay on the device.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account. This permanently deletes your account, username, backed-up game results, push tokens, notification preferences and notification log from our servers. The app then offers to delete the games stored on your device too.
  • Export your data or delete the data on your device in Settings → Account.
  • Turn notifications off in your device's settings.

For any other request about your information, including access or correction, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data on our servers is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account. A device's push token is removed when you sign out, or when Apple reports that the device no longer accepts notifications from the app.

Security

Your sign-in session is stored in your device's secure storage. All communication with our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On the server, access rules ensure that each account can read only its own data; only the leaderboard information described above is shared with other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Information
